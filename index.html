Equinox Night: stargazing for everyone

A free evening of telescopes, guided sky tours and hot chocolate. Designed from the start to work with a keyboard, a screen reader or a wheelchair.

Reserve a free ticket

About the night

Guided sky tours

Astronomers describe the sky out loud, so you can follow along without seeing the telescope.

Tactile star maps

Raised-print constellation maps and audio descriptions are available at the entrance.

Step-free site

Every telescope platform has a ramp, and seating is available along the whole route.

    Frequently asked questions

    What if it is cloudy?

    The talk and the tactile tour still go ahead indoors. We email everyone by 17:00 on the day.

    Can I bring my assistance dog?

    Yes. Water bowls and a quiet resting area are available near the entrance.

    Is there sign language interpretation?

    Yes, the 20:00 talk is interpreted into Spanish Sign Language.
